# Flight identification

Date context: today 2026-09-29. Photo posted on X at 10:30 PM on 2026-09-28.

## Verdict
**Starship Flight 14 (IFT-14), Booster 21 + Ship 41, Block 3 (V3), Starbase Pad 2 (OLP-2), 2026-09-28 12:48:59 UTC (7:48:59 am CDT).** Confidence about 85 percent.
First orbital Starship flight, 26 Starlink V3 satellites. Booster 21 and Ship 41 were expendable on this flight and were not caught. B21 made a controlled Gulf splashdown. Ship 41 reached 262x277 km at 32 deg.

## Photo clues (estimated from pixels; see photo-landmarks.json)
- Ship is uniformly black (RGB about 15,14,17). This is the tile-covered windward side facing the camera. The ship is not stainless.
- Booster is frosted and lit warm on its right edge. Sun is low, from the right of the frame.
- Horizon geometry: the camera looks roughly along the Boca Chica spit with the Gulf to the right and the lagoon to the left. Estimated look direction is about north to north-northwest, so the camera sits south of the pad. A right-hand (eastern) sun then matches a morning launch and does not match an evening launch (F11). Confidence in the look direction is moderate, about 60 percent.
- Tower has an open truss crown and arms parked wide, with one arm visible to the right. Its top is about level with the booster base at this instant, so the vehicle has just cleared the tower (T+about 6 to 8 s). Tower top is about 125 to 146 m. Consistent with Pad 2 (146 m tower cited generally; the Pad 2 chopsticks are about 10 m shorter than Pad 1's): https://www.nasaspaceflight.com/2025/08/starbase-pad-2-advancements-pad-1/ (search result; not fetched).
- Hot-stage and grid fin details cannot be resolved at this resolution (booster is only about 20 px wide). No V2-vs-V3 discrimination from grid fins is possible in the image. The identification therefore rests on lighting, timing and the same-day post.

## Caveats
- The Flight 13 or 12 possibility cannot be fully excluded without the source X post.
- Sunrise time on 2026-09-28 at Starbase (about 26 deg N) is estimated at about 07:12 CDT, so launch is about 36 min after sunrise and the sun elevation is about 6 deg (est).
